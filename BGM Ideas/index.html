Lakbay Salita soundtrack sketches

Seven looping background tracks, one per screen. They share Filipino-inspired instruments (kulintang gongs, bandurria plucks, bamboo marimba and bamboo flute), so the game sounds like one world while each screen keeps its own mood. Every loop restarts seamlessly.

These are synthesized drafts made in code, not recordings. They're meant to settle mood, tempo and instruments before you commission or license final music. Nothing here is in the Unity project yet.
