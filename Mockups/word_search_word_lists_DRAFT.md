# Word Search word lists - DRAFT for review (7 categories x 3 puzzles, 7-8 words each, with reused words)

Every category has three puzzles per language: Puzzle A has 8 words, Puzzle B has 8 words and Puzzle C has 7 words.
Each puzzle reuses 2 or 3 words that also appear in another puzzle of the same category (marked in the Note column), so a category needs only 19 different words (4 shared + 15 unique).
The 4 shared words are the most common ones in the category, so players meet them again.
Totals: 7 categories x 3 puzzles = 21 puzzles per language, 63 puzzles overall.
Words were taken from the app's dictionary files: the first meaning of the entry matches the English concept, and the word has 3-8 letters.
The dictionary often lists several spellings or dialect words for one meaning, so the **Word** column is the first candidate and **Other options** shows alternatives.
Please check each word with a speaker and replace any that are rare, a loanword, or a different dialect.
