Lakbay Salita sound effects

25 short sounds for buttons, typing and each game, made with the same bamboo, gong, pluck and bell tones as the music. Try them in context first, then go through the full list below.

Try typing: Wordle row

Type with your keyboard or tap the keypad. Enter checks the word (the answer is BALAY). A word that isn't 5 letters plays the "not a word" sound.

Try dragging: Word Search

Drag across the letters. Each new letter ticks a little higher. Find ISDA across the top row, or let go anywhere else to hear a miss.
